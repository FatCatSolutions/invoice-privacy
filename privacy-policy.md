# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** {PUBLISH_DATE}

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Invoice** app ("the App", "we", "us")
handles your information. The App is provided by FatCatSolutions ("the Developer").

We designed Invoice to keep your data on your device. The App has **no account
system**: we never ask you to sign up, and we never ask for your name, email,
phone number, or password to use it. We do not run a server that stores your
invoices, clients, or business details, and we do not build an advertising
profile about you.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter or create in the App is stored in the App's private storage
on your device, including:

- Your business profile, logo, tax details, numbering, and payment details (such
  as bank account details or a payment link)
- Your clients and their details (such as names, addresses, tax IDs, and notes)
- Your price book items
- Your invoices, estimates, receipts, and credit notes, their payments, and every
  PDF version you share
- Job photos you take or choose and attach to a document
- Signatures captured on the device (a client's signature on an estimate, your
  own on an invoice)
- App settings, such as your trial reminder and backup preferences
- A random install ID generated on your device (used only locally, for example
  to recognize a reinstall); it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere

The App works offline. This data stays on your device unless you choose to send it
elsewhere (see section 2). It is removed when you delete the App, unless you keep a
backup or export.

Client details you enter may be personal data about your own customers. You decide
what to enter and are responsible for it; we have no access to it.

## 2. Data that leaves your device, only when you choose

### Sharing and exports

When you share a PDF, or export your data as an app file, CSV files, or a PDF
archive, the App hands the file to your device's share sheet. The file goes only
where you send it (for example, email, messaging, or a files app), under that
service's own privacy policy. We never receive it.

### Optional backup to iCloud or Google Drive

Backup is **off until you turn it on**. Until then, the App makes no backup
network call and asks for no sign-in.

- **iPhone:** backups are stored in the App's iCloud container in your own iCloud
  account. No separate sign-in is needed.
- **Android:** backups are stored in the App's hidden app-data folder in your own
  Google Drive. Turning backup on asks you to sign in with Google and grant access
  to that folder only (the `drive.appdata` permission). If you decline, backup
  stays off.

A backup is one full copy of your App data (including photos, signatures, your
logo, and PDFs). It runs once a day on Wi-Fi while charging, or when you tap
"Back up now". Each new backup replaces the previous one after it finishes
uploading. The App does not add its own encryption to backups; they are protected
by Apple's or Google's own storage security under their privacy policies. We
cannot access your backups.

Apple or Google may store your backup outside your country. If local rules require
you to keep business records in your country (for example, New Zealand's IRD
rules), check before turning backup on; the export file is an alternative.

Turning backup off stops future backups. The last backup stays in your iCloud or
Google Drive until you delete it there. On Android, you can also remove the App's
access to your Google Drive in your Google account settings.

## 3. Diagnostics

The App uses **Sentry** to collect crash and error reports so we can fix bugs.
These reports may include your device model, operating-system version, App
version, and technical diagnostic data (such as stack traces). They are not used
to identify you.

The App currently collects no analytics about how you use it. If we add
analytics in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

## 4. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by **Apple** (App Store) or **Google** (Google Play).
The App never processes payments, and we never receive or store your payment-card
details. Billing, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by the App Store or
Google Play under their own privacy policies. The App asks the store for prices
and for your subscription status.

## 5. Device permissions

- **Camera:** used only when you tap "Take photo" to attach a job photo to a
  document. Photos are stored on your device.
- **Photos:** when you choose photos or a logo from your library, the App keeps a
  copy of only the images you select.
- **Notifications:** used only for the optional trial reminder, a notification on
  your device 2 days before your trial ends. It is scheduled on your device;
  you can turn it off on the paywall or in your device settings.

You can deny or withdraw any of these permissions in your device settings. The rest
of the App keeps working.

## 6. Third parties we share data with

| Recipient | Purpose | Their policy |
|---|---|---|
| Sentry | Crash and error diagnostics | sentry.io/privacy |
| Apple / Google | Process subscription purchases | apple.com / google.com policies |
| Apple (iCloud) / Google (Drive) | Store your backup, only if you turn backup on | apple.com / google.com policies |

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do **not** use advertising or
cross-app tracking SDKs.

## 7. Data retention

- On-device data: kept until you delete it or uninstall the App. The App never
  deletes your documents automatically.
- Backups: kept in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete them there.
- Crash reports: retained according to Sentry's data-retention policy.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the EU/UK GDPR or California CCPA), you may
have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to
or restrict its processing. Because we do not maintain accounts and your data lives
on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly by editing,
exporting, or deleting your data in the App, deleting your backups in iCloud or
Google Drive, or uninstalling the App. For anything else, contact us at the address
below and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is a business tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required
in your country). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us
and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, and backups use
Apple's or Google's own secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is
completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. Keep
your device protected with a passcode, since anyone who can open the App can see
your data.
