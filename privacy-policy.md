# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what the Invoice app ("the App", "we", "us") does with your information. FatCatSolutions ("the Developer") provides the App.

Invoice keeps your data on your device. There is no account system, so you never sign up and never give us your name, email, phone number, or a password. We don't run a server that stores your invoices, clients, or business details, and we don't build advertising profiles.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter or create lives in the App's private storage on your device:

- Your business profile, logo, tax details, numbering, and payment details, such as bank account details or a payment link
- Your clients and their details: names, addresses, tax IDs, and notes
- Your price book items
- Your invoices, estimates, receipts, and credit notes, their payments, and every PDF version you share
- Job photos you take or pick and attach to a document
- Signatures captured on the device, either a client's on an estimate or your own on an invoice
- App settings, such as your trial reminder and backup preferences
- A random install ID generated on your device. It is used only locally (for example, to recognize a reinstall) and is never sent anywhere

The App works offline. This data stays on your device unless you send it somewhere yourself (see section 2). Deleting the App removes it, unless you kept a backup or export.

Your client details may be personal data about your own customers. You decide what to enter and you are responsible for it. We can't see it.

## 2. Data that leaves your device, only when you choose

### Sharing and exports

When you share a PDF, or export your data as an app file, CSV files, or a PDF archive, the App passes the file to your device's share sheet. It goes only where you send it, such as email, a messaging app, or a files app, and that service's privacy policy applies. We never receive it.

### Optional backup to iCloud or Google Drive

Backup is off until you turn it on. Until then the App makes no backup network calls and asks for no sign-in.

- iPhone: backups go to the App's iCloud container in your own iCloud account. You don't need a separate sign-in.
- Android: backups go to the App's hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. Turning backup on asks you to sign in with Google and allow access to that folder only (the `drive.appdata` permission). If you decline, backup stays off.

A backup is a full copy of your App data, including photos, signatures, your logo, and PDFs. It runs once a day on Wi-Fi while the device charges, or when you tap "Back up now". Each new backup replaces the previous one once it finishes uploading. The App adds no encryption of its own, so backups rely on Apple's or Google's storage security and fall under their privacy policies. We can't access your backups.

Apple or Google may store your backup outside your country. Some countries require business records to stay inside the country (New Zealand's IRD rules are one example). If that applies to you, check before turning backup on. The export file is an alternative.

Turning backup off stops future backups. The last backup stays in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete it there. On Android you can also remove the App's access to your Google Drive in your Google account settings.

## 3. Diagnostics

The App uses Sentry to collect crash and error reports so we can fix bugs. A report may include your device model, operating-system version, App version, and technical details such as stack traces. We don't use reports to identify you.

The App collects no analytics about how you use it. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

## 4. Subscriptions

Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes subscriptions. The App never handles payments and we never receive or store your card details. The store takes care of billing, renewals, and refunds under its own privacy policy. The App only asks the store for prices and your subscription status.

## 5. Device permissions

- Camera: used only when you tap "Take photo" to attach a job photo to a document. The photo is stored on your device.
- Photos: when you pick photos or a logo from your library, the App keeps a copy of only the images you select.
- Notifications: used only for the optional trial reminder, which shows a notification on your device 2 days before the trial ends. The device schedules it, and you can turn it off on the paywall or in your device settings.

You can deny or withdraw any of these permissions in your device settings. The rest of the App keeps working.

## 6. Third parties we share data with

| Recipient | Purpose | Their policy |
|---|---|---|
| Sentry | Crash and error diagnostics | sentry.io/privacy |
| Apple / Google | Process subscription purchases | apple.com / google.com policies |
| Apple (iCloud) / Google (Drive) | Store your backup, only if you turn backup on | apple.com / google.com policies |

We don't sell your personal data, and the App has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

## 7. Data retention

- On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. The App never deletes your documents on its own.
- Backups stay in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete them there.
- Crash reports are kept under Sentry's data-retention policy.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. We keep no accounts and your data is on your device, so you can do most of this yourself: edit, export, or delete your data in the App, delete your backups in iCloud or Google Drive, or uninstall the App. For anything else, email us at the address below and we will answer within the time applicable law requires.

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is a business tool and is not aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

Your data sits in the App's private storage on your device, and backups use Apple's or Google's own secure storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. Protect your device with a passcode, because anyone who can open the App can see your data.
