## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up. Your invoices, clients, and settings are stored on your phone.

### How does the trial work?
New subscribers can start a 14-day trial, then a yearly or monthly subscription begins automatically unless you cancel. Leave **Remind me 2 days before my trial ends** on and the App sends you a notification before you're charged.

### I'm not getting the trial reminder.
Make sure notifications are allowed for Invoice in your device settings, and that the reminder was switched on when you started your trial.

### How do I restore my subscription?
Open the paywall, or tap your plan at the top of Settings, and tap **Restore purchases**. Your subscription is tied to your App Store or Google Play account, and doesn't carry over between iPhone and Android.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime from your store account: on **iOS** via Settings &rarr; your name &rarr; Subscriptions, or on **Android** via the Play Store &rarr; Subscriptions. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period.

### What happens to my data if I stop subscribing?
Everything you made stays on your phone. You can still view, share, and export it; you need a subscription to create or issue new documents.

### How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings &rarr; Backup and turn on backup to iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android). The App then makes a full backup once a day on Wi-Fi while charging, and you can tap **Back up now** at any time.

### How do I move to a new phone?
Turn on backup on your old phone, then choose **Already use the app? Restore or import** on the new phone's first screen. You can also export an app file from Settings &rarr; Export and import it on the new phone, including between iPhone and Android.

### Does the App check my invoices are legal?
The App follows the invoice rules for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand and warns you about common problems, but it doesn't give tax or legal advice. You are responsible for your own tax compliance. See our [Terms &amp; Conditions](terms-and-conditions).
