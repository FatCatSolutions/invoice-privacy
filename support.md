## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up. Your invoices, clients, and settings stay on your phone.

### How does the trial work?
New subscribers can start a 14-day trial. When it ends, a yearly or monthly subscription begins unless you cancel first. If you leave "Remind me 2 days before my trial ends" switched on, the app sends you a notification before you're charged.

### I'm not getting the trial reminder.
Check that notifications are allowed for Veli in your device settings, and that the reminder was switched on when you started the trial.

### How do I restore my subscription?
Open the paywall, or tap your plan at the top of Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, so it doesn't carry over between iPhone and Android.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel from your store account. On iPhone, go to Settings &rarr; your name &rarr; Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store &rarr; Subscriptions. The subscription stays active until the current billing period ends.

### What happens to my data if I stop subscribing?
Everything you made stays on your phone. You can still view, share, export, and back it up. Creating or issuing new documents needs a subscription.

### How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings &rarr; Backup and turn on backup to iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android). The app then backs up once a day on Wi-Fi while your phone charges. Tap Back up now to do it at any time.

### How do I move to a new phone?
Turn on backup on the old phone. On the new one, choose "Already use the app? Restore or import" on the first screen. You can also export an app file from Settings &rarr; Export and import it on the new phone, even if you're switching between iPhone and Android.

### Does the app check that my invoices are legal?
Partly. It follows the invoice rules for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand, and warns you about common problems. It doesn't give tax or legal advice, and your tax compliance is your responsibility. See the [Terms &amp; Conditions](terms-and-conditions).
